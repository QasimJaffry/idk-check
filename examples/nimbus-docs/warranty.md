# Warranty

Every Nimbus bike comes with a 5-year warranty on the frame and a 2-year warranty on the motor and battery.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects. It does not cover damage from crashes, racing, or water submersion.

To make a warranty claim, email support@nimbus.example with your order number and a photo of the problem. Claims are answered within 3 business days.

The warranty is transferable once, to a second owner, if the original owner registers the transfer in the Nimbus app.
