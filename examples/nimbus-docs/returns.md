# Returns and refunds

You can return a bike within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as it has been ridden less than 100 km.

Return shipping is free in the UK and costs £49 elsewhere in Europe.

Accessories can be returned within 14 days if unused and in their original packaging.

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 business days of the bike reaching our warehouse in Bristol.
