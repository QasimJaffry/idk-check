# Battery and charging

A full charge takes about 4 hours with the standard 4 A charger. The optional fast charger takes about 2.5 hours.

Store the battery between 10 °C and 25 °C. Do not charge it below 0 °C.

The battery keeps at least 80% of its capacity for 800 full charge cycles.

Nimbus does not sell batteries with a larger capacity than 500 Wh.
