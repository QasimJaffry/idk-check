# Models

Nimbus sells three bikes: the City, the Trail and the Cargo.

The City weighs 18 kg, has a range of up to 80 km, and costs £1,890.

The Trail has full suspension, a range of up to 65 km, and costs £2,650.

The Cargo can carry up to 120 kg including the rider, has a range of up to 70 km, and costs £3,200.

All three use the same 500 Wh battery, so batteries are interchangeable between models.
