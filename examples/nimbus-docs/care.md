# Nimbus Care plans

Nimbus Care is an optional service plan. There are two tiers.

Care Basic costs £8 per month and includes one annual service and free brake pads.

Care Plus costs £15 per month and adds theft insurance and a replacement bike within 48 hours if yours is stolen.

You can cancel either plan at any time from the Nimbus app; cancellation takes effect at the end of the billing month.
